Why Use Mechanical Keyboards?

This website is for information about mechanical keyboards, mechanical keyboards are, a popular hobby built around customizing and building your preferred keyboard. These keyboards use individual mechanical switches under every key. Mechanical keyboards are loved for their durability, satisfying feeling, sound, and wide range of ways you can personalize them.
